About

Three decades at the same bench.

A family workshop, not a showroom chain.

N.D.I. began as one man, one van and a bandsaw. Three decades later the same principle runs the business: the person who measures your room is the person who draws it, and the workshop that cuts it is fifteen minutes from your house.

We don't sell flat-packs and we don't subcontract the fitting. Fewer hands touch the work, which is why the joints line up and why the number you called still answers after the invoice is paid.
