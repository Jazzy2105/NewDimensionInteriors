About N.D.I.

A family workshop, not a showroom chain.

N.D.I. has been making kitchens and built-in cupboards for three decades. The person who measures your room is the person who draws it, and the workshop that cuts it is fifteen minutes from your house.

That's the whole proposition: fewer hands, better joints, and a phone number that answers after the invoice is paid.
